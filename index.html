CONFIDENCE, WITH CONTEXT

Trust should be explained. Not assumed.

Clear information helps you choose a teacher with confidence. Here’s what our approach means.

A note for parents

For learners under 18, a parent or guardian should manage bookings and help coordinate sessions.

Read the Privacy notice →

Documents, not just promises.

Identity and teaching credentials submitted by teachers are reviewed before a Verified badge is displayed. Verification is a document check, not a guarantee of learning outcomes.

Reviews from actual sessions.

Only students with a completed booking can leave a verified student review. Read individual experiences alongside subjects, credentials and your own learning needs.

Your information deserves care.

We use booking details to coordinate your session. Personal documents are not shown on public profiles. The Privacy notice explains what is collected, why and how to request help.
